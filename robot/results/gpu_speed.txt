GPU world (MuJoCo Warp 3.17, one H100 PCIe), measured 2026-10-27 with gpu_speed.py:
each copy runs one 50-step experiment from a random start with random actions (the most contacts).
   1536 copies:    6.5 s = 237 experiments a second
   4096 copies:    9.5 s = 432 experiments a second
  16384 copies:   23.1 s = 710 experiments a second
Physics alone (no controller), recorded as one graph: about 1,010 experiments a second at 16,384 copies.
Without recording graphs it was 3 to 5 times slower; fewer solver iterations did not help.
Most contacts at once: 17 a copy (room 256); most constraint rows in a copy: 236 (room 1024).

Against our CPU copy (test_gpu.py), one control step: median 0.02 mm on demo steps (contact),
0.0004 mm on random steps; largest 5.7 mm and 0.46 mm.

median 0.49 mm (99%: 1 cm), the same gap as between the two MuJoCo versions.
